# 04 — Design Semantic Transistors

*Corresponds to Phase 3 (design) and Section 5, Section 8 of [The Student Experience.md](../../The%20Student%20Experience.md).*

## Goal

Design — on paper, before writing the semantic authority record itself — the "transistor" your responsibility will be: the smallest coherent unit of one declared obligation, one focused responsibility, and one authoritative signal.

```text
Signal:
code-body-has-canonical-lineage

Question:
Does this observed body resolve to exactly one admitted
feature → scenario → obligation → responsibility chain?

GREEN:
Exactly one current authority chain owns the body.

RED:
No chain, multiple chains, or an incomplete chain owns the body.
```

## What "transistor" means here

```text
One obligation:
Every admitted body must have one canonical lineage.

One responsibility:
Resolve the canonical lineage of one observed body.

One signal:
GREEN or RED.
```

The metaphor matters: a transistor is the smallest unit that reliably switches between two states under one clear condition. A responsibility that can't be described this simply is not ready for the next phase — it's still a circuit pretending to be a transistor.

## What you produce

A short design note in this folder describing the GREEN/RED shape above, before you touch `semantic-authority/` in the next phase. This is the last checkpoint before the design becomes a durable authority record.
